Campus Discipline

Hostel Rules & Guidelines

Ensuring safety, mutual respect, and a peaceful study atmosphere for all residents.

Curfew

🚪 Gate Timings

Main hostel gates close strictly at 10:00 PM. Entry register must be signed for return after 9:30 PM. Late arrival requires prior written warden sanction.

Visitors

👥 Guest Policy

Visitors and day scholars are permitted in the designated visitor lounge only between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM. Overnight guest stays are strictly prohibited.

Safety

⚡ Electrical Appliances

High-wattage appliances (immersion rods, induction heaters, heavy coils >500W) are forbidden inside rooms to protect building circuitry.

Study Hours

🤫 Silence Hours

Strict silence hours are observed from 10:30 PM to 6:00 AM to promote study and rest. Loud music and corridor disruptions are prohibited.
